Frequently asked questions

How much does a Skill cost to run?

Most Skills run fully local at zero token. The few that need to read or judge content spend only a small amount, so a Skill runs at a low and predictable cost with no subscription and no per use fee.

Does my data leave my network when a Skill runs?

No. A Skill runs on your own machine, so the data it processes stays inside your own network.
