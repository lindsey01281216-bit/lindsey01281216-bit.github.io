What a Company System can do

A Company System is not a single ERP or CRM. It is a set of local pipelines joined into 1 operating and reporting system for the whole company: the daily, weekly, and monthly reports, a live board that refreshes on a schedule, the product lifecycle view, and the new client onboarding report in 1 place, most of it running locally at zero token so the numbers stay inside your own network. Frequently asked questions

Is a Company System the same as an ERP or CRM?

No. A Company System is a set of local pipelines joined into 1 operating and reporting system, holding the daily, weekly, and monthly reports and a live board in 1 place, rather than a single off the shelf product.

How much does a Company System cost to run?

Most pipelines run locally at zero token, and only the few that read or judge content spend a small amount, so the running cost stays predictable and the numbers stay inside your own network.
